# Code of conduct

Fetchly is a small open source project, and everyone who takes part (opening issues, discussing ideas, sending
pull requests) is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Keep discussion about the work: criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming someone meant harm.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and that a "no" is about the change, not you.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a request after it has been declined

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository
for behavior that goes against this code of conduct. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ggvolta) or with a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/ggvolta/fetchly/security/advisories/new) if it's sensitive. Reports are
handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
